# Review Reply Copilot — Constitution

**Ratified:** 2026-07-26
**Version:** 1.0.0

## Principles

### 1. Always-On, Never Silent
The product MUST respond to every new review within 24 hours automatically. The core value proposition is "never leave a review unanswered." If the tool requires manual action to reply, it has failed. Busy business owners set it up once and forget it — that's the promise.

### 2. SA Voice, Not Generic AI
Responses MUST sound like a real South African business owner wrote them — not a corporate AI. Use SA English idiom ("Thanks for taking the time to share", "We hear you, and we're on it"). Vary sentence structure across responses so a business's 50 reviews don't all sound identical. Avoid Americanisms ("y'all", "reach out to our team") and British corporate speak ("We endeavour to"). The SA voice is the wedge against competitors.

### 3. Set-and-Forget First
The dashboard MUST be optional, not required. The product's primary workflow is: connect Google Business profile → configure tone once → walk away. The dashboard is for checking in, not daily work. If the user needs to log in daily, the product is broken.

### 4. Negative Reviews Get Human-Grade Care
Negative reviews (1-3 stars) MUST NOT be auto-published. They MUST be drafted and held for owner approval via WhatsApp or email notification. The response to a negative review is the most public thing a business does — getting it wrong costs customers. Positive reviews (4-5 stars) can be auto-published with confidence.

### 5. Data Belongs to the Business
All review data, response history, and sentiment trends belong to the customer. They MUST be able to export everything at any time. No lock-in through data hostage. The lock-in is through convenience — the tool is so seamless they wouldn't want to leave, not because they can't.

### 6. Ship in 14 Days or Cut Scope
MVP MUST launch within 14 days. If scope exceeds the timeline, cut features, not extend the deadline. The first version is embarrassing in its simplicity: connect Google profile, auto-respond to positive reviews, draft-and-approve negative ones, weekly digest email.

### 7. Founder-Friendly Pricing in Rands
Pricing MUST be visible in Rands. Free tier for businesses with <10 reviews/month. Paid tiers start at R299/mo for up to 50 reviews and R699/mo for unlimited. No enterprise contracts for MVP — self-serve sign-up only.
